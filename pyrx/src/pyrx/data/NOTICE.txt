C7_E1B.txt and C8_E1C.txt are the Galileo E1-B and E1-C primary codes (fifty codes each,
4092 chips as 1023 hexadecimal digits per line), as attached to

    European GNSS (Galileo) Open Service Signal-In-Space Interface Control Document,
    Issue 2.2, November 2025. (c) European Union 2025.
    https://www.gsc-europa.eu

They are reproduced here unmodified. OS-SIS-ICD-2.2-terms-and-annex-H.pdf holds, as
published and unmodified, the pages of that document that govern them: its "Terms of Use
and Disclaimers" (pp. i-ii) and its Annex H, "Authorisation Concerning the OS SIS ICD IPRs"
(pp. 105-114). Those terms ask that both be passed on, whole, with any part of the
document.

The codes are Technical Data of that document, owned by the European Union. Annex H
allows their use and storage royalty-free, the source acknowledged, on its own terms and
limits, which each user accepts for themselves; what it grants cannot be transferred or
